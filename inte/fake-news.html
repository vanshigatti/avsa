UK, 2020 · WhatsApp & Facebook · 50M+ shares

5G is radio frequency technology. Radio waves are non-ionising electromagnetic radiation — they physically cannot carry, transport, or replicate biological entities like viruses. Crucially, many countries with zero 5G infrastructure had severe COVID outbreaks, while some heavily 5G-equipped countries controlled it well. The claim is impossible at a fundamental scientific level.
The truth
COVID-19 spreads through respiratory droplets and close contact. Protections are masks, ventilation, vaccination, and hand hygiene — confirmed by thousands of peer-reviewed studies. 5G is simply the 5th generation of mobile network technology operating on higher radio frequencies than 4G.
⚠️ 77 UK cell towers were set on fire. Telecom engineers were physically attacked. This is among the costliest pieces of misinformation in recent history.

DEBUNKEDChemtrails from aeroplanes are chemical or biological agents being sprayed on the population by governments
USA, 1990s · YouTube · 80M+ cumulative views globally
Why it's false
Aircraft contrails (condensation trails) form when hot humid exhaust from jet engines meets cold air at altitude — the same process as your breath fogging on a cold day. Contrail persistence depends on atmospheric humidity. A 2016 survey of 77 atmospheric scientists found 77 of 77 identified the claimed "evidence" as normal contrails. Making this conspiracy real would require the simultaneous complicity of all airlines, airports, aircraft engineers, fuel suppliers, and meteorologists worldwide — across countries that are active enemies of each other.
The truth
Contrails consist of water ice crystals from engine exhaust. Duration and spread depend on relative humidity at altitude: in humid air they persist and spread; in dry air they dissipate quickly. This variability — what conspiracy theorists point to as "evidence" — is simple atmospheric physics. Real geoengineering research (stratospheric aerosol injection) is conducted openly and published in academic journals.
⚠️ Has led to harassment of airline and meteorology personnel. Contributes to distrust of aviation and atmospheric science. Related beliefs correlate with rejection of other scientific consensus.
